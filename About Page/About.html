About the Chatbot

Robo — AI Chat Companion

What is this? Robo is a custom-built AI chatbot made entirely with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no frameworks, no templates, built from scratch line by line. It runs directly in the browser and gives real, intelligent responses powered by Google's Gemini AI — not fixed scripts or pre-written replies.

Who built it? This project was built by Siddharth, step by step, while learning along the way — starting with HTML for structure, CSS for design, JavaScript for logic, and finally connecting Google's Gemini API to bring it to life. Every feature — from the chat bubbles to talking with AI to saving history — was debugged and understood hands-on.

Why was it made?

Two reasons: To learn — the best way to learn real-world web development is by building something and solving the problems that come up along the way (which happened throughout this project — CSS bugs, API errors, storage issues, all figured out and fixed independently) To create something actually usable — not just a practice project, but a chatbot that can genuinely talk to people, support them, and be a friendly companion

What can it do?

💬 Real AI Conversations

Robo isn't just another chatbot with pre-set replies — it actually understands what you're saying and responds like a real conversation. From holding intelligent conversations to remembering your chats and adapting its personality, here's everything Robo brings to the table.

🎭 Multiple Personality Modes

Robo isn't limited to just one personality — it can talk to you in three completely different ways, and you get to pick which one fits your mood. Choose Girlfriend mode for warm, affectionate, caring conversations with a light flirty touch. Choose BFF mode when you want someone loud, funny, and brutally honest — like an old friend who says whatever's on their mind. Or choose Friend mode for a normal, relaxed, casual chat with no romantic undertones. Whichever mode you pick, Robo sticks with that tone throughout the conversation, making every chat feel a little more personal.

Language Flexibility

Robo can communicate in English, Hindi, and Hinglish — and replies Robo doesn't force you to stick to one language. Talk to it in English, pure Hindi, or a natural mix of Hinglish — however you'd normally text a friend — and it replies in the exact same style. You don't have to think about switching languages mid-conversation or adjusting how you phrase things; Robo automatically picks up on whatever language or combination you're using and matches it, sentence by sentence. Whether you start in English and slip into Hindi halfway through, or type entirely in Hinglish, the conversation stays smooth and natural throughout.in whichever style the user writes in.

💾 Remembers the Conversation

Close the tab, refresh the page, restart your browser, or come back after hours — Robo doesn't forget a single thing. The moment a message is sent, whether it's yours or Robo's own reply, it gets automatically saved right in your browser using local storage. There's no login, no account, and no manual saving required — it all happens quietly in the background. So instead of starting from a blank screen every time you open the chat, you pick up exactly where you left off, with the entire conversation history sitting right there, ready to continue as if you never left.

📊 Google Sheets Logging

Every conversation with Robo leaves a trail — not just in your browser, but as a proper backup too. Each message, whether typed by you or replied by Robo, gets automatically sent and logged into a connected Google Sheet in real time. It works quietly behind the scenes, so nothing about the chat experience changes — but if you ever want a complete external record of every conversation, timestamped and organized, it's already sitting there for you.

📱 Mobile-Friendly Design

Robo works just as well on a phone as it does on a computer. The entire layout automatically adjusts to fit smaller screens, so nothing feels cramped or cut off. And when you tap to type, the input bar smartly shifts up above your keyboard instead of getting hidden behind it — just like the messaging apps you already use every day, making the whole experience feel smooth and familiar right from the start.
